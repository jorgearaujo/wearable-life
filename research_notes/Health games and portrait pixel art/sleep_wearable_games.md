# Games and gamified apps driven by sleep, recovery, heart-rate or wearable data

State as of 11 November 2026. One section per product or product group, then a cross-cutting section on score anxiety, then a list of candidates that were not covered.

How to read the sourcing:

- A plain link means I opened the page and the claim is on it.
- "(search snippet)" means the claim comes from a search-result summary of that page and I did not open the page itself. Treat these as weaker.
- Anything under "Inferences" is my reasoning, not a sourced fact.
- Anything under "Gaps" could not be verified. Nothing has been filled in from memory.

## Pokémon Sleep (and Pokémon GO Plus +)

### Takeaway
The largest product in this space (30 million downloads, about $235 million lifetime spend) rewards sleep length and regularity, deliberately does not reward any particular bedtime, and has no penalty for a missed night beyond tired helper Pokémon. Its most repeated complaints are practical: phone on the mattress, battery drain and lost sessions. It added passive import from Apple Health and Health Connect in September 2024.

### Cited Findings

Data and how it is read
- Tracks sleep with the phone's microphone and accelerometer; the optional Pokémon GO Plus + accessory tracks sleep without a phone nearby and plays bedtime reminders — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)
- Smartwatches were added as a tracking method in September 2024 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)
- The 2024 update covered Apple Watch (through Apple Health on iOS) and Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch and Fitbit (through Health Connect on Android) — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/apps/pokemon-sleep-now-supports-smartwatches-for-more-accurate-tracking-152113397.html) (search snippet)
- Garmin support began 13 March 2026, through Apple Health on iOS or through Garmin Connect to Health Connect on Android — [Pokémon GO Hub](https://pokemongohub.net/post/sleep/pokemon-sleep-is-now-compatible-with-garmin-smartwatches/) (search snippet)
- Smartwatch sleep that was manually edited may not be accepted, and sleep recorded before the link was made cannot be registered — [official portal notice](https://ph.portal-pokemon.com/apps/pokemon_sleep_now_lets_you_track_your_sleep_with_your_smartwatch.html) (search snippet; I could not tell which of the listed pages carried this wording)
- The game still runs on the phone; it cannot be used on the watch itself — same snippet as above

How data changes the game
- Sleep is sorted into three types (Dozing, Snoozing, Slumbering). The dominant type sets which Pokémon appear. Sleep type and noise produce a Sleep Score; Drowsy Power is Sleep Score multiplied by Snorlax's strength, and higher Drowsy Power brings more visiting Pokémon — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)
- A score of 100 needs about 8.5 hours asleep; sessions can be split, and sessions under 90 minutes are not accepted — [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Sleep/archives/418707) (search snippet)

Design intent (primary source)
- "We intentionally did not design the app around rewarding specific sleep schedules, such as 'early bird' or 'night owl,'" because that could push players to stay up late or cut sleep short to catch particular Pokémon — [The Pokémon Company, "Why We Created Pokémon Sleep", 28 Sep 2023](https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/topics/detail/110.html)
- The team dropped two earlier ideas: a "Pokémon Day Care" that raised Pokémon through sleep, and nightly videos of Pokémon visiting, the latter because watching videos on waking might keep people in bed — [The Pokémon Company](https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/topics/detail/110.html)
- The stated aim was an app "that tracks sleep and makes waking up every morning a joy", under a "sleep-first" philosophy, supervised by sleep researcher Dr. Masashi Yanagisawa (S'UIMIN inc.) — [The Pokémon Company](https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/topics/detail/110.html)
- In-app Sleep Tips teach ideas such as social jet lag and that sleep cannot be stored in advance — [The Pokémon Company](https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/topics/detail/t-9/)
- Each island's music has morning, afternoon and night variations — [The Pokémon Company](https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/topics/detail/t-9/)
- A 2023 Famitsu developer interview says the concept took about five and a half years, and discusses keeping play sessions short because it is a sleep-measuring app — [Famitsu, July 2023](https://www.famitsu.com/news/202307/21310083.html) (search snippet, Japanese)

Missed nights and missing data
- If tracking failed or was forgotten, sleep can be added by hand. Manual entries can reach back up to 30 hours, restore helper Pokémon Energy according to duration and add one bonus biscuit, but do not run "sleep research" (no Pokémon encounters) — [SmartScope summary](https://smartscope.blog/en/blog/pok-mon-sleep-emergency-maintenance-end-time-delay-2026/) (search snippet); a guide exists at [SeekingTech](https://seekingtech.com/how-to-manually-add-sleep-data-in-pokemon-sleep/)
- Sources disagree on the reward for manual entry: Bulbapedia says a small reward for the first session of the day; SeekingTech says nothing beyond the daily login reward — [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep) vs [SeekingTech](https://seekingtech.com/how-to-manually-add-sleep-data-in-pokemon-sleep/) (both search snippets; Bulbapedia returned 403 when opened)
- A user reported that putting the phone down before the sleep-mode confirmation appears can lose a whole night of data, leaving Pokémon too tired the next day — [search result set](https://seekingtech.com/how-to-fix-pokemon-sleep-tracking-issues/) (search snippet, single user account, exact page uncertain)

Business, team, status
- Free with in-app purchases; developed by Select Button, published by The Pokémon Company; released 17 to 20 July 2023 by region — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)
- Development moved in 2024 to The Pokémon Works (joint venture of The Pokémon Company and ILCA) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep); discussed in [Famitsu, Nov 2024](https://www.famitsu.com/article/202411/23904) (search snippet)
- A Premium Pass at about $10 a month grants extra sleep points and a premium shop — [Digital Trends, 27 Jul 2023](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/pokemon-sleep-one-week-later/)
- Alive and updated as of late 2026: a new area, Cyan Beach (Expert), began 10 August 2026 — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokmon-sleep-surpasses-230m-in-three-years/)

Numbers
- Over 28 million downloads and over one billion sleep sessions by July 2025; 30 million downloads by April 2026 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)
- $234.9 million lifetime player spending at the third anniversary (July 2026). Year one close to $100 million; year three $68.2 million, down 3.2% year on year; about $5.7 million a month on average; best day $543,000 on 9 June 2026. Estimates are AppMagic's — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokmon-sleep-surpasses-230m-in-three-years/)
- 3.7% of Pokémon mobile in-app revenue in 2026 so far, a distant third behind Pokémon GO and TCG Pocket (AppMagic) — [mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-q1s-top-performers-milestones-for-pokemon-tcg-pocket-heartopia-efootball-and-plenty-more/) (search snippet)
- US weekly downloads fell from about 15K to about 8K over Q2 2025 — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-pokemon%20collector%20games-revenue-us-6089902328293296048c7c1b) (search snippet)

Player and press reaction
- A reviewer was "cautious of turning good sleep into something I feel I should be rewarded for", said sleep became "a precious resource to be managed", noted a partner and a cat distorted readings, and objected to paid shortcuts: "there are no shortcuts when it comes to your health" — [Digital Trends, 27 Jul 2023](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/pokemon-sleep-one-week-later/)
- The same reviewer praised the social side (sharing nightly reports with friends) — [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/pokemon-sleep-one-week-later/)
- A TechRadar columnist wrote that sleeping next to the phone led to "some of the worst nights I've ever had" because they kept checking progress — [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/gaming/my-sleep-has-gotten-worse-since-playing-pokemon-sleep-but-i-still-wont-stop) (search snippet; the page body did not load when opened)
- Players on the subreddit reported battery drained in four to five hours with the session lost; others objected to the advice to charge the phone on the bed as a fire risk — [Dot Esports](https://vip-develop.dotesports.com/?p=914240) and [Gamepur](https://gamepur.com/?p=534227) (search snippets; which article carried which claim is uncertain)
- Version 1.0.4 reduced power use while tracking — [Bulbagarden forum](https://bulbagarden.net/threads/pokemon-sleep-updates-to-version-1-0-4-update-resolves-several-ongoing-bugs-as-well-as-reducing-power-consumption-while-sleep-tracking.298110/) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- The wearable import arrived 14 months after launch, and Garmin 32 months after. For a game built on Health Connect and HealthKit from day one, this is a head start on the part Pokémon Sleep found hardest.
- The refusal to accept edited sleep records and records from before linking is an anti-cheat rule. A game with no leaderboard, no purchases tied to sleep and no data leaving the device does not need it.
- Manual entry that restores energy but skips the "research" reward is a working model for "data not arrived": the day is not lost, but the full reveal waits for real data.
- Revenue fell only 3.2% in year three, which suggests a sleep game can hold a paying audience for years on a once-a-morning loop.

### Gaps
- No CEDEC or GDC talk by Select Button or The Pokémon Company on Pokémon Sleep was found in English or Japanese searches. The Famitsu interviews are the nearest thing and were not opened.
- No published retention figures were found.
- What exactly happens to Snorlax and the weekly cycle after several missed nights was not confirmed.
- How the game treats smartwatch data that arrives hours after waking was not found.
- Pokémon GO Plus + price, sales and its Pokémon GO behaviour were not researched.

## SleepTown (Seekrtech, makers of Forest)

### Takeaway
A bedtime-commitment app, not a sleep tracker: keep to your bedtime and wake time without touching the phone and a building is added to your town; fail and the building under construction is destroyed. It is the clearest loss-based design in the set and appears to be unmaintained since late 2023.

### Cited Findings
- You set a bedtime and wake goal and must open the app before bedtime to start a building; miss the bedtime and "you cannot construct a building". Using the phone during sleep time destroys the building under construction. On waking you shake the phone to finish — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleeptown/id1210251567)
- Up to two days off a week can be set, and failing on a day off does not end the streak; consecutive successful days earn extra coins — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleeptown/id1210251567)
- Can "sync your sleep data to Apple's HealthKit" (it writes; the listing does not say it reads) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleeptown/id1210251567)
- $1.99 one-time on iOS, 4.6 from 1.5K ratings (US store); last update version 3.4.1 on 23 November 2023 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleeptown/id1210251567)
- Privacy label lists email linked to the user for analytics, plus usage and diagnostics data — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleeptown/id1210251567)
- Reviews: one cites "the frustration of a house collapsing" as the motivator; another asks for a setting to leave the app for five minutes without losing the building — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleeptown/id1210251567)
- One reviewer says that after missing bedtime they "immediately lose all motivation to actually go to sleep"; another lost two buildings in a row because the alarm did not sound — [App Store / Apptail listings](https://apptail.io/app/sleeptown-mSxb/ireland) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- Missing the bedtime removes the reason to go to bed at all that night. This is the opposite of what a sleep game wants, and a direct argument for never gating the night's reward on a deadline.
- No update in three years while still on sale suggests it is abandoned, though I found no statement saying so.

### Gaps
- Android status, download numbers and any Seekrtech statement on the app's future were not found.
- How phone use is detected is not stated on the listing.

## Sleepagotchi (Gotchi Labs)

### Takeaway
Started in 2022 to 2023 as a reward-only sleep game with a room to decorate, became a Solana "sleep-to-earn" gacha battler, and in May 2026 pivoted away from the game towards an AI wellness coach. Useful mainly as a cautionary path.

### Cited Findings
- Early design (January 2023, iOS only): going to bed on time or sleeping enough earns loot boxes with bedroom decorations; gamification should "reward only positive behaviour and not punish bad"; could link to Apple Watch sleep data — [MeinMMO, 18 Jan 2023](https://mein-mmo.de/en/the-sleepagotchi-app-rewards-you-with-loot-boxes-when-you-go-to-bed-on-time-aims-to-improve-gamers-sleep,935597)
- Integrates with Apple Health for Watch-based and sleep-mode tracking; advertised as working with or without wearables — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6743857605) (search snippet)
- Later version: care for Dino, decorate rooms, collect heroes through gacha and send them into automatic battles while asleep — [review summary](https://apptail.io/app/sleepagotchi-sleep-game-mtG/egypt) (search snippet)
- Reviews split: some say it got them to bed on time, others that the added game systems crowded out the simple tracker; 3.0 stars from 34 ratings on one listing in October 2026 — [aggregator](https://apptail.io/app/sleepagotchi-sleep-game-mtG/egypt) (search snippet)
- May 2026: pivot from gamified sleep to a broader AI health and wellness platform, starting with an AI Sleep Coach — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/sleepagotchi-pivots-from-sleep-to-an-ai-based-health-and-wellness-platform/) (search snippet; page returned 403)
- Claims 2.2 million all-time users and 78,688 daily active users — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/sleepagotchi-catches-2-2m-users-for-its-ai-sleep-coach/) (search snippet)
- $3.5 million seed, a further $6.5 million reported for the AI plan (single source), token renamed from SLEEP to CHI with no launch date — [eGamers](https://egamers.io/sleepagotchi-and-parent-gotchi-labs-land-spots-in-welcome3s-invite-only-network/) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- User counts for crypto reward apps are inflated by people farming tokens, so 2.2 million is not comparable with Pokémon Sleep's downloads.
- The review complaint (the game crowded out the simple thing) is a warning about piling systems on a once-a-day loop.

### Gaps
- "Shleep" as a separate product: nothing found. It may be a misremembered name.
- Whether the original game is still playable after the pivot was not confirmed.

## Finch (self-care pet)

### Takeaway
The model for a non-punishing pet: the bird never suffers when you skip a day. It runs on goals the user ticks off by hand; I found no evidence that it reads health data.

### Cited Findings
- You set your own goals and each completed goal gives the bird energy for adventures; free tier has the core features, optional subscription — [WhistleOut review](https://www.whistleout.com/CellPhones/Apps/finch-self-care-app-review) (search snippet)
- "Skip a day, and the bird doesn't suffer"; a missed day feels like a pause — [Headway review](https://makeheadway.com/blog/finch-app-review/) (search snippet)
- The Finch team added an App Pause feature so users can step away without losing their daily streak — [Finch team post on r/finch](https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/finch/comments/1k1ik78/app_pause/mnomnpl/?context=3) (search snippet)
- An analysis of 40,294 reviews found complaints about lost streaks and lost accounts, for example a user reset to day one after a 234-day streak — [Appbot](https://appbot.co/blog/finch-app-reviews-emotional-attachment-user-retention-product-loyalty/) (search snippet)
- The App Store listing describes goals, journaling, breathing and a sleep section but does not mention HealthKit — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1528595748) (search snippet)
- Developer is Finch Care Public Benefit Corporation — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1528595748) (search snippet)
- Revenue claim of about $1 million a month, $12 million a year, founders Stephanie Yuan and Thomas Budi — [Medium post](https://medium.com/@jashsak/the-12m-mental-health-app-that-broke-every-silicon-valley-rule-bd9d06e725f8) (search snippet; unverified secondary source)
- Widgets and sharing are credited for retention and growth — [Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/x0hd2ssr80y5n7gv0w967pg7hwd7tl) (search snippet, not opened)

### Inferences
- Even in an app famous for kindness, the one thing that can be lost (the streak number) is what people complain about. A game where nothing can drop avoids this class of complaint entirely.

### Gaps
- Whether Finch reads any Apple Health or Health Connect data (for example steps for a walking goal) could not be confirmed either way after three searches. Treat it as manual entry unless the help centre says otherwise.
- No primary-source revenue, download or retention figures.

## Fitbit Ace LTE (Bit Valley and the eejie) and Fitbit pet clock faces

### Takeaway
Google's children's watch ties play time and a customisable creature to movement, with strong privacy limits. It is the nearest big-company example of "creature plus home reflects your activity", but for steps only and behind a subscription.

### Cited Findings
- Announced 29 May 2024, on sale 5 June 2024, $229.95, ages 7 and up; Ace Pass at $9.99 a month or $119.99 a year is required for LTE and the Fitbit Arcade — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/fitbit-ace-lte-smartwatch-kids/)
- "The more they move, the more game time they unlock"; a "Noodle" ring celebrates reaching the daily move goal — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/fitbit-ace-lte-smartwatch-kids/)
- Eejies become "healthy and happy" as movement goals are met; arcade tickets from activity buy outfits and decor for the eejie's home in Bit Valley; physical bands unlock items — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/fitbit-ace-lte-smartwatch-kids/)
- Privacy: only parents see activity and location; location deleted after 24 hours, activity data after at most 35 days; no ads or third-party apps — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/fitbit-ace-lte-smartwatch-kids/)
- Still being sold in March 2026, at a record low of $99.95 — [deal article](https://www.pulse.bot/hardware/news/the-kid-friendly-fitbit-ace-is-100-which-matches-its-best-price-bb665063-8ef2-4898-9469-6cdcfd0a9aa8) (search snippet)
- Fitbit Ace 3 (2021) has animated clock-face characters that "change and grow as kids make progress toward their goal throughout the day"; animated faces cost battery life — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/fitbit/ace-3/) (search snippet)
- The Fitbit Ionic had dog and cat clock faces where the pet could be fed after a set number of steps — [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/wearables/smartwatches-should-add-virtual-pets-like-tamagotchi-for-fitness-motivation) (search snippet; attribution to this article is my best match)

### Inferences
- The eejie is described only in positive terms; the launch post never says what an inactive day looks like. Either nothing bad happens or Google chose not to show it.
- A fall from $229.95 to $99.95 suggests weak sales, but that is a guess.

### Gaps
- Status in November 2026: no discontinuation notice found, and no confirmation that it is still sold.
- Whether the eejie looks sad or unwell after inactive days.
- The Ace LTE does not appear to use sleep in its game; not confirmed.

## Small Apple Watch and iPhone pets that read HealthKit

### Takeaway
There is a crowd of tiny step-fed pets, almost all by solo developers, and almost none use sleep. One 2026 app, Habitvs, is very close to this project's rules.

### Cited Findings

Habitvs (pet "Kova"), the closest match found
- Reads, with permission: sleep, steps, activity rings, HRV, heart rate and resting heart rate, mindfulness, daylight and workouts; Apple Watch optional — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762097932)
- These "shape Kova's energy and mood": a stronger day makes the pet livelier, a slower day gives "a calmer response" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762097932)
- Quiet days do not remove progress; "grace weeks" protect streaks, and its Journey mode has no streak to break — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762097932)
- Tapping the pet's state badge shows the recovery score, today's signals and what it takes to reach the next state — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762097932)
- A morning recovery suggestion appears when last night's sleep score is under 50; an "overreaching" alert was revised to need a sustained signal over several days to cut false alarms — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762097932)
- Privacy label "Data Not Collected"; health data "stays on your device"; chat with the pet runs on-device — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762097932)
- Free, solo developer (Igor Barbosa Maciel), copyright 2026, about 16 updates between May and October, too few ratings to display — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762097932)

Others
- StepDog: reads step count through HealthKit; the dog eats, plays and curls up to sleep once the step goal is met — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1474625303) (search snippet)
- nomi: paid, Watch-only; reads steps and active energy; says the data never leaves the device — [App Store](https://apple.co/3gnvFHW) (search snippet)
- Tamawatchi: a native Watch pet fed by steps; first creature free, others 99 cents — [Korben](https://korben.info/en/tamawatchi-native-apple-watch-tamagotchi-steps.html) (search snippet)
- WatchieBesti: reacts to steps; the pet gets sleepy if you sit still; one-time purchase — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760113059) (search snippet)
- Hatchiko: the pet sleeps when the Watch enters sleep mode — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/bs/app/hatchiko/id6744754447) (search snippet; the US page returned 404)
- Teddy: Sleep Tracker Pet (Offbeat Apps): the companion levels up and gets "happier and healthier" the more you sleep; tracks with Apple Watch or phone by the pillow; subscription; 8 ratings; last update August 2024 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teddy-sleep-tracker-pet/id6526465688)

### Inferences
- Habitvs shows the "pet reflects sleep, HRV and activity, nothing is lost, everything on device" idea is being tried on iOS. It differs from this project in showing a recovery score and alerts to the player, in having no world, and in being iOS only. It has no visible traction yet.
- Habitvs's change to require a multi-day signal before alerting is evidence that single-night HRV signals produce false alarms in practice.
- The small apps mostly sell for a one-time price and have little or no audience, which says the step-pet idea alone does not carry a product.

### Gaps
- Pixel Pals and Standland: no evidence found that either drives its pet from health data; not confirmed either way.
- No download or revenue figures for any of these.
- Habitvs version dates carry no year on the listing; 2026 is assumed from the copyright line.

## Indie games driven by sleep or wearable data

### Takeaway
A handful exist, all tiny. The notable ones are Slumber Drift (HealthKit sleep moves an airship, fully offline) and Garmigotchi (a Garmin watch-face pet that can die). No Health Connect sleep game by an indie was found, and nothing built directly on the Oura or Whoop APIs.

### Cited Findings
- Slumber Drift (Takumi Kanzaki, released 19 March 2026): "your sleep powers the wind"; deeper rest carries the airship further across four sky zones, with encounters, crystals and equipment; a daily routine of about two minutes; fully offline, no servers, no subscription — [developer site](https://www.tknzk.app/)
- Daybound Tower (same developer, 17 February 2026): steps, stairs and workouts become floors of a tower, one run a day — [developer site](https://www.tknzk.app/)
- Garmigotchi (Garmin watch face, January 2026): reads Body Battery, stress, sleep score, steps and heart rate; mood comes from a Body Battery by stress grid, recalculated each minute; "neglect points" rise with low battery, poor sleep, long stress and missed step goals, and at 16 points the creature dies and is a ghost until next month; it evolves through five stages over the days of the month — [DEV Community post](https://dev.to/jestersimpps/garmigotchi-a-tamagotchi-that-lives-off-your-health-data-1m6d)
- Sleep Hero: Bedtime Habit Game: a sleep RPG with quests and battles; developer active in October 2025 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sleep-hero-bedtime-habit-game/id6743801693) (search snippet)
- Dream Adventure (Tokyo Game Show 2023 Indie 80): measures sleep time, snoring and sleep efficiency and ties sleep quality to progress — [TGS programme](https://expo.nikkeibp.co.jp/tgs/2023/en/program/indie/indie80/rpg.html) (search snippet)
- Fitscape: an activity RPG that says it can also use sleep and recovery data — [scape.fit](https://scape.fit/apple-health-fitness-game/) (search snippet)
- SleepQuest: an Android tool on itch.io — [itch.io](https://sossio4.itch.io/sleepquest) (appeared in results, not opened)
- A 2025 review in Frontiers in Sleep says evidence that gamification improves sleep habits is still limited — [Frontiers in Sleep](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sleep/articles/10.3389/frsle.2025.1607117/pdf) (search snippet)
- An academic paper, "Designing Games for Healthy Sleep", exists — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/341697604_Designing_Games_for_Healthy_Sleep) (appeared in results, not opened)

### Inferences
- Garmigotchi is the anti-pattern for this project: it grades the player on hidden values (stress, Body Battery) and kills the pet. Its monthly reset is still a neat way to bound loss.
- Slumber Drift's "distance travelled" is progress that only grows, with a bad night meaning a shorter trip, not a loss. That fits "the level never drops". What a bad night shows is not stated on the site.
- The field on Android is open: every sleep-driven indie found is iOS or Garmin.

### Gaps
- No ratings, downloads or reviews for any of these.
- No Oura API, Whoop API or Health Connect indie game found; absence in two searches is not proof.

## Gentler Streak (Gentler Stories)

### Takeaway
The reference for a non-punishing activity app: rest days keep the streak alive, and the user can declare sick, injured or on holiday. Even so, some users dislike its streak reminders.

### Cited Findings
- "Keep your streak alive by following your body's needs, even when it calls for rest days"; status can be changed for illness, injury or vacation to "skip feeling guilty" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)
- Reads HealthKit: sleep, steps, HRV, resting heart rate, wrist temperature, VO2 max, workout heart rate; says data stays on the device — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)
- The privacy label nonetheless lists purchases, coarse location, identifiers, usage and diagnostics as collected (not linked to the user) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)
- Free with Premium from $8.99 monthly to $179.99 lifetime; 4.7 from 8.8K ratings; version 5.13.5 released 22 October — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)
- Awards listed: 2024 Apple Design Award (Social Impact), 2022 Apple Watch App of the Year — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102); Apple's page: [Apple Design Awards 2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024) (not opened)

## Recovery scores: Oura, Whoop, Bevel, Athlytic

### Takeaway
These apps show the player exactly what this project hides: a morning number built from HRV and resting heart rate. Oura's wording ("Pay attention") and its Rest Mode are the most relevant details; hard evidence on how users react is thin and mostly journalistic.

### Cited Findings
- Oura Readiness bands: 85 and up "Optimal", 70 to 84 "Good", under 70 "Pay attention, you're not fully recovered" — [Oura](https://ouraring.com/blog/readiness-score/) (search snippet)
- Oura Rest Mode: no daily activity goal is given and the activity score and its contributors are hidden; meant for illness, injury or jet lag — [Oura support glossary](https://support.ouraring.com/hc/lv/articles/5949130374547-Glossary) (search snippet)
- Oura says its insight messages "often focus on rest, rather than overexertion" — [Oura blog](https://ouraring.com/blog/inside-the-ring-the-story-behind-ouras-daily-insight-messages/) (search snippet)
- A March 2026 Harper's Bazaar column describes checking Oura scores within seconds of waking and letting them set the day; psychologist Rachel Goldman: "When people check a readiness score before they've even checked in with themselves, it can shift everything"; Michael Breus: "It has made sleep into something you have to perform and score", and advises weekly trends over single nights — [AOL / Harper's Bazaar, 26 Mar 2026](https://www.aol.com/articles/oura-ring-judging-160543237.html)
- The same column says Oura added "more compassionate messaging" in 2025 to address score anxiety — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/oura-ring-judging-160543237.html) (not confirmed against an Oura source; its statement that Rest Mode was new in 2025 looks doubtful)
- Whoop's own study linked surveys to 7.9 million days of data and found HRV and resting heart rate track self-reported stress, anxiety and depression; it did not study reactions to the score — [Whoop press release](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/press-center/new-whoop-research-finds-compelling-links-between-mental-and-physical-health/) (search snippet)
- A journalist living by Whoop for a week described wanting to be "in the green" and treating recovery as a win — [Stylist](https://stylist.co.uk/fitness-health/fitness-tracker-strain-recovery-advice/615976) (search snippet)
- Articles describe a loop of bad score, body scanning, anxiety, worse sleep, more tracking, and advise treating the device as "a smoke alarm, not a judge" — [Plunge blog](https://plunge.com/blogs/blog/is-your-recovery-score-lying-to-you) and [InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/daily_brief/health-and-fitness/fitness-wearables-nocebo) (search snippets; commercial and lifestyle sources)
- Bevel's recovery score combines HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and the previous day's strain; a low score is framed as "a lighter day will yield better results" — [Lifestack review](https://lifestack.ai/blog/bevel-app-review) (search snippet)
- Apple's sleep score on Apple Watch was described as deliberately not gamified the way activity rings are — [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3516947532749702) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- Oura's Rest Mode is the same idea as this project's rest mode: on a bad day, remove the target instead of showing a failed one. Oura makes the user switch it on; here it can be automatic.
- The expert advice (trends over single nights; check in with yourself before the number) supports showing three soft meters and a world, not a daily score.
- Bevel's phrasing shows a low value can be worded as a suggestion for today. That matches the rule that every change is explainable in one plain sentence.

### Gaps
- No first-person Reddit threads were retrieved; sentiment here is from journalists and bloggers.
- Athlytic: nothing specific found beyond a one-line comparison.
- No published data on how often users abandon these apps because of bad scores.

## Score anxiety and orthosomnia: the evidence

### Takeaway
"Orthosomnia" comes from a 2017 case series, not a trial. The best experimental evidence is that false negative sleep feedback made people with insomnia feel worse during the day, while a one-off bad score did nothing measurable to healthy sleepers.

### Cited Findings
- Baron et al. 2017, Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine, "Orthosomnia: Are Some Patients Taking the Quantified Self Too Far?": case reports of patients preoccupied with perfecting tracker data — [PMC5263088](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5263088) (search snippet)
- Orthosomnia is not a recognised disorder and the evidence is small case studies — [Sleep Foundation](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/sleep-tracking/orthosomnia) (search snippet)
- Gavriloff et al. 2018, Journal of Sleep Research: 63 people with insomnia given sham positive or negative sleep-efficiency feedback; the negative group reported lower alertness (d = 0.79) and more sleepiness and fatigue (d = 0.55); objective vigilance did not differ — [Oxford publication record](https://www.psy.ox.ac.uk/publication/854408) (search snippet)
- Robson, Ellis and Elder 2022, Sleep and Biological Rhythms: 54 healthy sleepers given a false good or poor sleep score once; no significant difference in pre-sleep arousal or subjective sleep; authors say repeated feedback needs study — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41105-022-00390-9) (search snippet); [PMC10899903](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10899903/)
- A study in Cognitive Research found people told (falsely) that they slept poorly did worse on cognitive tests — cited in [AOL / Harper's Bazaar](https://www.aol.com/articles/oura-ring-judging-160543237.html) as PMC7078271 (not opened; details unverified)
- A 2021 study found trackers identified deep sleep correctly only 59% of the time — [Sleep Foundation](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/sleep-tracking/orthosomnia) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- The harm is concentrated in anxious or poor sleepers, who are also a likely audience for a calm sleep game. Designing for them (no number, no red, no verdict on waking) costs healthy sleepers nothing.
- Sleep-stage accuracy is weak enough that stages should stay an optional input, which matches rule 4.

### Gaps
- No controlled study of daily, repeated score feedback over weeks was found.
- No study of game-style sleep feedback (as opposed to a number) was found.

## Nintendo: Vitality Sensor, Quality of Life sleep device, Alarmo

### Takeaway
Nintendo twice cancelled body-sensing products because the sensing was not reliable enough, then shipped a sleep-adjacent product (Alarmo) that treats its sleep records as a light extra.

### Cited Findings
- Wii Vitality Sensor: Iwata said in 2013 it worked for only about 9 in 10 people and "was of narrower application than we had originally thought" — [Engadget, 5 Jul 2013](https://www.engadget.com/2013-07-05-the-wii-vitality-sensor-is-dead-at-least-for-now.html) and [TechRadar](https://techradar.com/news/gaming/nintendo-finally-explains-why-it-killed-the-wii-vitality-sensor-1164072) (search snippets)
- Quality of Life sleep-and-fatigue sensor: in February 2016 Kimishima said Nintendo did not have the conviction it could become a product, while continuing to study the field — [TechCrunch, 4 Feb 2016](https://techcrunch.com/2016/02/04/nintendo-hits-snooze-on-plan-to-introduce-a-sleep-tracking-device) (search snippet)
- Alarmo's motion sensor "works remarkably well" but cannot tell a partner or pet from the user; best for people who sleep alone — [Nintendo Life](https://nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-sound-clock-alarmo-pricey-but-delightful-and-something-only-nintendo-could-pull-off) and [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/nintendo-sound-clock-alarmo-review) (search snippets)
- Alarmo Sleep Records show time in bed and time taken to get up; reviewers call them basic and sometimes wrong (one showed over 12 hours in bed); records are kept up to a year — [Nintendo Life](https://nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-sound-clock-alarmo-pricey-but-delightful-and-something-only-nintendo-could-pull-off), [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/nintendo-sound-clock-alarmo-review), [Digitec](https://www.digitec.ch/en/page/nintendo-alarmo-review-better-than-expected-35289) (search snippets)

### Inferences
- "Works for 9 in 10" was not good enough for Nintendo. A game that must work with only steps and sleep duration avoids the same trap, because those two are available to nearly everyone.
- Alarmo's records are forgiven for being wrong because nothing depends on them. The more a game's outcome hangs on a reading, the more its errors hurt.

### Gaps
- Alarmo sales figures and current availability not checked.
