# Art references

Reference images for art direction. The images in this folder are not committed (other people's work, direction only); this file is.

One line per image: the file name, and what you like or dislike about it.

All `pixel-style/` and `vertical-framing/` images are Cast n Chill (the lead reference); `character/` images are Neko Odyssey. Measurements below were taken from 1920×1080 screenshots and are approximate.

## 1. Overall pixel style

| File | What to take from it | What to ignore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pixel-style/style-autumn-cabin.jpg` | The look to ship: dense autumn foliage, warm palette, mirror reflections, cabin with chimney smoke | Fishing-specific props |
| `pixel-style/time-day-clear-deer.jpg` | Clear-day palette, crisp reflections, clustered foliage shading | Outboard motor boat |
| `pixel-style/light-golden-coastline.jpg` | Painterly clouds and big sky built from pixel clusters | Coastal setting |

What these show:

- **Pixel size:** native resolution is about 384×216, scaled 5× to 1080p. In the portrait crops (section 2) that is roughly 120 art pixels wide by 216 tall, so chunkier than the spike's 180-wide view.
- **Outlines:** none. Shapes are separated by colour and value; dark edges appear only where there is real shadow.
- **Colours:** many, not a strict limited palette. Sky gradients, translucent fog and tinted reflections suggest colour grading and lighting layers on top of the base art. (JPEG compression prevents an exact count.)
- **Shading:** textured, not flat. Foliage is built from irregular clusters of 2–4 pixels; water uses short horizontal streaks for reflections and shimmer. Little or no checkerboard dithering.

What these show:

- **Side-on, straight camera.** No top-down angle.
- **Horizon at roughly mid-screen; sky in the top third or more.** The sky carries the sun, moon and weather.
- **Character and dog in the lower third,** standing out against the water or a quiet background.
- **Character size:** about 25 art pixels tall in a 216-pixel-tall view, so roughly 12% of the screen height. The spike's 24-pixel character in a 320-tall view is about 7.5%. The crops suggest a character closer to 12% feels right.

## 3. The same scene at different times and weather

| File | What to take from it | What to ignore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pixel-style/time-day-clear-deer.jpg` | Day: bright, high contrast, natural colours | — |
| `pixel-style/time-sunrise-fog.jpg` | Morning fog: low mist hugging the water, sun low, pink and violet palette | — |
| `pixel-style/time-dusk-waterfall.jpg` | Dusk: the whole scene shifts to purple shadows and pink light | Busy composition |
| `pixel-style/time-night-aurora.jpg` | Night: deep blues, compressed values, bright accents (moon, stars) | Aurora as a constant (a rare event at most) |
| `pixel-style/time-winter-frozen.jpg` | Winter good day: snow-laden trees, frozen waterfall, soft pink sky | The pop-up menu; the underwater cut-away view |
| `pixel-style/light-golden-coastline.jpg` | An in-between weather day that is still beautiful: heavy clouds with golden light breaking through | — |

How far night and dusk depart from day: **a long way.** Dusk is not day with orange added; the whole scene is re-coloured into a narrow pink-purple range. Night is not day darkened; it becomes almost monochrome blue with a few bright accents. Hue shifts matter more than brightness changes.

## 4. Light at night

| File | What to take from it | What to ignore |

| `pixel-style/time-sunrise-fog.jpg` | The sun's glow: a few concentric flat bands, no dithering noise | — |
| `pixel-style/time-night-aurora.jpg` | The moon's soft halo against a dark sky | — |

What these show: the lead reference uses **banded glows with a few clean flat bands, not dithered ones.** The same treatment should suit a campfire or lit window.

## 5. Character

| File | What to take from it | What to ignore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pixel-style/style-autumn-cabin.jpg` | Base character and dog style: small figure that belongs in the landscape, at the world's pixel size, no outline | Their exact look (our character and dog are original designs) |
| `pixel-style/time-winter-frozen.jpg` | Winter outfit at small size; a big dog nearly as tall as the seated character still reads clearly | Same as above |
| `character/character-neko-market.jpg` | Charm; a distinctive silhouette with a signature item (backpack); animals at the same scale as the character | Outfit, hair, face detail, anime proportions; 3D urban setting |
| `character/character-neko-street.jpg` | A companion animal walking alongside; a small character inside a wide scene | Same as above |

What these show:

- **Cast n Chill's character is about 25 art pixels tall.** The face is only a few pixels and has no drawn eyes; the hat and clothing colours carry the identity.
- **The dog is large:** close to the character's height, with a strong, readable shape.
- **Interactions are fully animated.** When the player pets the dog, the character walks over, reaches down and pets its head while the dog wags its tail and leans in; a small heart can appear on top. Cast n Chill shows only a heart and a hand cursor, which is not the bar here.

## Gaps to fill

- **3:** the same location at several times of day and weather (Cast n Chill's time-of-day change lets you capture one spot at every time). Rain, storm, wind and blizzard.
- **4:** a campfire or lit cabin window at night.
- **5:** animation: a short GIF of idle, walking, chopping, and especially a character petting a dog.
- **6:** a real dormant/flourishing pair. Outside games is fine: frost on a garden, a cabin with smoke curling on a still winter morning.
- **7:** a positive example of meters and a one-sentence explanation sitting on a scene.
- **Supporting references:** Kingdom Two Crowns (seasons and weather on one place), Celeste (character motion, campfire).
